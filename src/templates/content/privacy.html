What we collect

When you order, we collect your name, contact details, delivery address and the photos and text you send us to personalise your products. Payments are handled by our payment partner; we never see or store your full card details.

How we use it

  • To print, pack and deliver your order and keep you updated about it.
  • To help you with questions, reprints and refunds.
  • To send offers, only if you've joined the Kissa Club. You can unsubscribe anytime.

Your photos

Your photos are used only to make your order. We delete them from our systems within 30 days of delivery unless you ask us to keep them for a re-order. We never share or publish customer photos without permission.
