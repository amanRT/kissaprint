Why we started KissaPrints

Our phones hold thousands of photos, yet the moments that matter most rarely leave the camera roll. KissaPrints began with a simple idea: every photo has a kissa - a story - and stories deserve to be held, gifted and kept.

We design and print personalised keepsakes in India, from polaroid-style Memoroids to song plaques, star maps and magazines. We obsess over the details that make a gift feel personal: thick card, honest colour, careful packing and a real person checking every order.

How we work

  • Human-checked. Every photo is reviewed before printing, so blurry or dark shots get flagged - not printed.
  • Printed with care. Premium papers, rich inks and hand-finishing on every piece.
  • Packed to be gifted. Every order leaves our studio ready to hand over.
