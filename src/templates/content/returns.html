Draft policy - review with your team before launch.

Personalised means made for you

Because every KissaPrints product is printed with your photos and words, we can't accept returns or exchanges for change of mind. We do, however, stand behind every single print.

Our reprint promise

If your order arrives damaged, misprinted or with something missing, send us a photo of the product and packaging within 48 hours of delivery. We'll reprint and reship it free of charge, or refund you if a reprint isn't possible.

Cancellations

You can cancel for a full refund until your order goes to print - usually within a few hours of ordering. Once printing has started we can't cancel the order.

Refund timelines

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5-7 working days.

Tell us what happened - we'll make it right.
